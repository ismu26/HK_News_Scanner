# Relevance criteria (plain language; edit freely)

Version: v1, 9 Nov 2026. Every change gets a dated line in "Change history" below.

## Who the list is for

A Partnerships Intern on Marketnode's Hong Kong team. The list should surface stories worth
reading and possibly acting on: new products, regulator moves, partner or competitor news,
and openings to start a conversation.

Marketnode context used for judging (from Ishaan's notes; not independently verified):
- Tokenization: tokenizing and distributing funds, debt and commodities (for example a
  Singapore-vaulted gold fund with Lion Global Investors); possibly structured products (to confirm).
- AI: Smartflow, which reads credit agreements and books deal data into Finastra's Loan IQ.
- Investors and partners named so far: SGX Group, Temasek, HSBC, Euroclear, Stellar Development
  Foundation, Finastra, Finloop, Lion Global Investors.
- Regulators that matter most: HKMA, SFC, FSTB (Hong Kong); MAS (Singapore).

## Relevant

Concrete news with a clear outcome and direct implications for Marketnode. Usually at least one of:
- A specific launch, deal, partnership, pilot, licence, investment or regulatory decision.
- Named firms, products, regulators or platforms.
- Dates, figures or other verifiable detail.
- A direct link to Marketnode's business: tokenized funds, debt, commodities or structured
  products; fund distribution; AI for credit or loan operations; its investors, partners or
  competitors; Hong Kong or Singapore regulation.

## Not relevant (or low relevance)

- Forecasts and opinion without new facts ("tokenization will take off in two years").
- Crypto price moves, meme coins, exchange marketing.
- Generic AI hype with no financial-services application.
- Old news repackaged.

## Scoring scale (0 to 5)

- **5**: Concrete event squarely in Marketnode's lanes AND touching its investors, partners,
  competitors, or HK/SG regulators. Example: the JQQQ launch (see labels).
- **4**: Concrete event squarely in Marketnode's lanes with an APAC link or a named major player,
  but no direct Marketnode connection.
- **3**: Concrete event in an adjacent area (stablecoins, tokenized deposits, settlement
  infrastructure, AI in HK/SG bank or fund operations), OR credible survey data (label it
  "survey data").
- **2**: Concrete but tangential (crypto exchange licensing, non-APAC news with little product relevance).
- **1**: Forecast or opinion only; generic AI news.
- **0**: Noise: prices, meme coins, marketing, old news resurfaced.

Only scores of 3 and above make the daily list (at most 10 a day).

## Facts vs forecasts (long documents and reports)

Rank in this order: named pilots, deals, figures, dates and regulatory steps (facts) >
survey data (middle tier, labelled "survey data") > forecasts and opinion.
